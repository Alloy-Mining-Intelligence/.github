# Alloy Mining Intelligence

Industrial AI for mining and mineral processing.

We build software that helps mining operators make better decisions from the data their plants already
produce — connecting existing process historians and control systems to models that surface what is
happening in a circuit and what to do about it.

## Working with us

Most of our repositories are private. If you are here about a specific engagement, your usual contact
is the right place to start.

- **Website:** [alloymining.ai](https://alloymining.ai)
- **Security:** please read our [security policy](https://github.com/Alloy-Mining-Intelligence/.github/blob/main/SECURITY.md)
  before reporting a vulnerability — do not open a public issue.
